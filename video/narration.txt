A11yClass is an offline accessibility auditor for a teacher's course page.

The intended learner is a student who uses a screen reader or keyboard only navigation. Their task is simple but essential. Find this week's reading and submit the assignment.

The barrier is invisible to many teachers. A page can look fine while headings, labels, links, contrast, focus order, and tables quietly block that student.

Watch the needs repair sample run. The auditor reads pasted HTML locally and reports checklist coverage, not a compliance certificate.

The first repairs are ranked. Each finding names the exact element, the severity, the learner impact, and a concrete fix.

The checklist table shows all eight checks. Borderline items are honest signals that need a person to confirm them.

Finally, the printable handoff gives a teacher something clear to bring to IT. Everything runs offline with synthetic examples and no student records.
